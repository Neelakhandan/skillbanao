heading: "The Next Designer Will Not Just Make Screens"
body: |
  Design is changing. AI can now generate ideas, interfaces, copy, flows, and even code. But AI still needs human judgement.

  The next generation of designers will need to:
skills:
  - "Understand users deeply"
  - "Frame the right problems"
  - "Translate research into decisions"
  - "Use AI as an assistant, not a replacement"
  - "Prompt with context and intent"
  - "Critique AI-generated outputs"
  - "Build and test working prototypes"
  - "Communicate design decisions clearly"
pull_quote: "AI can speed up execution. UX gives execution a reason."
