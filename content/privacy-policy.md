- Keep you logged into your account
- Remember your preferences and settings
- Record various aspects of your visit and assist us in providing you with uninterrupted service
- Understand how you interact with our website (through analytics tools)
- Measure the performance of our marketing campaigns

### Types of cookies we use:

- **Essential cookies** — required for the website and LMS to function properly
